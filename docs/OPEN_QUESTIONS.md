| OQ-009 | Does a custom effect (a `def` whose body imports `./x.c` and `./x.js`) load on all three engines of the pinned Bend (interpreter, C, JS), and what sub-millisecond precision does the JS side deliver? | S8 | `port/probes/clock/` (`main.bend`, `clock_wall.c`, `clock_wall.js`): `Clock.wall() -> IO(String)` answering `<epoch seconds>.<nine digits>` | RESOLVED 2026-09-20 under bend 2.0.20: it loads on all three. interpreter `1789884581.715000000`, C `1789884582.378291177`, JS `1789884582.638000000` (`date +%s.%N` said `1789884582.646053126`).
