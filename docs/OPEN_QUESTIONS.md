| OQ-001 | The original sorts through SQL `ORDER BY created_at` over TEXT. With mixed fraction widths (`…05Z`, `…05.1Z`, `…05.000000001Z`, an offset zone), is the order textual or chronological, and what breaks ties? | S6 (order), S4 (ready/list sort) | `edge_precision_ready`, `edge_precision_sort_created`, `edge_precision_list_json` (fixture `precision`) | RESOLVED: chronological. `edge_precision_ready.out` lists `proj-aae` (12:00:04+02:00 = 10:00:04Z), `proj-aaa` (`…05Z`), `proj-aac` (`…05.000000001Z`), `proj-aab` (`…05.100Z`), `proj-aad` (`…05.1Z`, equal instant: id ascending breaks the tie), then `aaf`, `aag`, `aah`. `--sort created_at` is newest first with the same id-ascending tie-break (`aah aag aaf aab aad aac aaa aae`) | 2026-09-20 |
| OQ-002 | When a mutation rewrites `issues.jsonl`, what happens to records it did not touch: unknown fields, `""` optionals, duplicate or unsorted labels, non-canonical timestamps, absent defaults? | S2 (load), S5 (write-back) | `edge_precision_rewrite` | RESOLVED from `edge_precision_rewrite.out`: every record is rewritten. `unknown_field` is dropped. `"description":""` and `"assignee":""` are dropped, while `"compacted_at_commit":""` is kept.
